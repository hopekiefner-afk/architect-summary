Invention Summary

This invention is a deterministic substrate which is a new category of computing that enforces rule-bound execution in regulated environments.

  • Deterministic Engine — Execution follows rule‑bound transitions rather than heuristics.
  • Schema Contract — Variability lives in domain schemas; the engine remains constitutional.
  • Replayable Audit — Every transition produces transcript‑grade truth.
  • Exception Governance — Exceptions are first‑class states with governed transitions.
  • ML Boundary — AI is advisory only; deterministic logic stays authoritative.
  • Category Scope — A new substrate for regulated workflows across finance, healthcare, government, manufacturing, and infrastructure.
